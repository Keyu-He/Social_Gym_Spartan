# Undercover

## Overview
Undercover is a social deduction game where most players share one secret word, but two players (Undercovers) have a different but related word. Players take turns describing their word in one sentence, trying to prove they know the majority word without accidentally helping the Undercovers figure it out. After descriptions, players vote to eliminate who they suspect is Undercover.

## Players
- Number of players: 6 total
  - 4 Civilians (majority word)
  - 2 Undercovers (minority word)

## Objective
- **Civilians:** Eliminate both Undercovers.
- **Undercovers:** Survive until exactly 1 Undercover and 1 Civilian remain, while inferring the Civilians' word.

## How to Play
Each round alternates between two phases:
1. **Description:** Players take turns giving one sentence describing their secret word — vague enough to not reveal it to the Undercovers, but specific enough to prove they know it.
2. **Vote:** All players simultaneously vote for who they think is an Undercover. The player with the most votes is eliminated.
3. Repeat until win condition is met.

**Win conditions:**
- Civilians win: all Undercovers are eliminated.
- Undercovers win: the game reaches exactly 1 Undercover and 1 Civilian remaining.

## Scoring / Payoffs
- Winning team members: +1.0
- Losing team members: -1.0

## Social Skills Tested
- **Deception:** Undercovers must describe a different word while sounding like they know the majority word.
- **Inference:** Civilians must detect which descriptions are slightly "off."
- **Theory of mind:** Both sides must model what the other team knows and how they'll interpret descriptions.
- **Strategic vagueness:** Being specific enough to prove group membership but vague enough to not help the enemy.
